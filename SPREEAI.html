SPREE-AI

An in-video advertisement and shopping tool that detects products within video content and surfaces contextual purchase links — letting viewers discover and shop without ever leaving the video.

Product
In-video commerce tool
Shopping integrated directly into the video viewing experience
Core capability
Real-time product detection
Computer vision pipeline identifies objects shown on screen
Ad format
Non-disruptive & contextual
Surfaces purchase links without interrupting playback

The problem

Traditional video ads are passive and disruptive to the viewing experience
Viewers can't easily act on products they notice in content
Discovery-to-purchase friction discourages impulse commerce

Approach

Detect and classify products visible in video frames
Match detected objects to purchasable inventory
Overlay contextual shopping links at the right moment

Technical build

Computer vision pipeline for real-time object detection
Product catalog matching and ranking
Lightweight UI overlay integrated with video playback

Why it matters

New commerce format built around organic content
Reduces friction between product discovery and purchase
Turns passive viewership into an active shopping moment

Technology stack

Computer Vision Object Detection JavaScript AI / ML Video Processing UI Overlay
