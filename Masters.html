Augmented Reality for Surgical Telementoring

Researcher

I contributed to an augmented reality surgical telementoring platform that enabled remote experts to guide procedures using a transparent display and in-field virtual annotations. The work focused on translating expert intent into intuitive overlays, reducing attention shifts, and validating the system for real surgical workflows including austere environments.

System
AR transparent display telementoring
Remote expertise delivered in the surgical field of view
Core capability
Virtual annotations aligned to anatomy
Real-time guidance without breaking sterile workflow
Use case
Surgical support in austere environments
Designed for constrained settings and rapid deployment

The problem

  • Remote experts cannot easily “point” or demonstrate precise guidance in real time
  • Surgical teams lose time and safety margin with frequent attention shifts
  • Austere environments increase the cost of limited expertise

What we built

  • Transparent AR display for in-field overlays
  • Virtual annotations anchored to the surgical scene
  • Interaction patterns designed for surgical constraints

How it was evaluated

  • Task-driven studies focused on accuracy and speed
  • Qualitative feedback on usability and workflow fit
  • Scenarios relevant to real operating conditions

Why it matters

  • Makes expert guidance available beyond major medical centers
  • Reduces cognitive burden by keeping guidance in view
  • Improves coordination and decision support under pressure

Technology stack

Augmented Reality • Transparent Displays • Scene Alignment • Computer Vision and Tracking • Real-Time Rendering • Human Factors and Usability Evaluation • Telemedicine Workflows
